Privacy Policy — Buzzer Challenge

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile app "Buzzer Challenge", published by Screalth Studios ("we", "us").

Buzzer Challenge is a local, offline timing game. This policy explains what data is involved when you play.

Data the app collects directly

Buzzer Challenge does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, or any personal information, and does not send your game data (scores, settings, target times) anywhere — everything is stored only on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Buzzer Challenge shows ads served by Google AdMob. To do this, AdMob and its partner networks may collect and use device identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA), approximate location, and other technical data to serve and measure ads, which may include personalized ads where you've given consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland) or permission (iOS App Tracking Transparency). You can control this:

  • iOS: via the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: via Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (wording varies by Android version).
  • EEA/UK/Switzerland: via the consent form shown in-app, which you can reopen anytime from Settings → Privacy Choices.

See Google's privacy policy for how it handles this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

In-app purchases

Buzzer Challenge offers an optional one-time "Pro" purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS); we never see or receive your payment details. The app only asks the store whether you own Pro, so it can unlock the features and remove ads, including when you restore purchases on a new device. We run no servers and keep no purchase records of our own.

Permissions

The app requests vibration access on Android, used only for haptic feedback during play — no data is collected through it.

Children

Buzzer Challenge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes; the "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest version.
