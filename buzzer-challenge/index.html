Buzzer Challenge

A minimal timing game. Set a target time, tap the big buzzer to start a blind timer with no numbers shown, tap again to stop, and see how close you got.

Features

  • Choose any target time, down to the hundredth of a second.
  • Multi-tap rounds: split the target across several play/pause taps.
  • Local pass-and-play for 2 or more players, each with their own neon colour.
  • No account needed, and your scores stay on your device.
  • Optional one-time Pro unlock that removes ads and adds extra colours, players and settings.
