Delete your account data

ElementTD does not have its own username and password accounts. Online features use a random anonymous ID, and sign-in with Google Play Games is optional. The data we store for you is:

  • your cloud-saved progress (currencies, upgrades, unlocked levels, card collection), stored with Google's Saved Games API if you linked Google Play Games;
  • your display name, Arena and Challenge standings, published defense and match history, stored on our servers.

To delete this data

  1. Open ElementTD.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Cloud Save Data and confirm.

This permanently deletes your cloud-saved progress and your server-side data. Your local, on-device progress is unaffected unless you also clear the app's data or uninstall it.

If you can't open the app, email screalth@gmail.com and we'll help you delete your data.
